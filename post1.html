I have been thinking for quite some time about starting my own website-blog in order to share and archive my writing. I am not sure in which language I will do so; I might interchange between Greek and English. This idea of mine about creating a website came out of a feeling of necessity and a deep fascination I had. About the fascination: sometimes I would "mindlessly" (I don't believe it is so mindless...) search around the web and find people's personal blogs and websites from years ago... I was always intrigued by this; the web in my mind is an enormous library where everyone's contribution sits, void of time (also there is probably mostly pornography in this "library" but anyway). A Blogspot I would commonly visit is https://theinfinite.blogspot.com/?m=1; this is a big part of my inspiration. About the necessity: I believe that a human's ability to write and to immortalise his ideas is the reason that we were able to prevail. The web is what typography was to writing in stone, but with the rise of the internet, people use formats within other applications, like Twitter, Facebook, etc. I believe that these formats take away the text's immortality that is usually granted from the web. Also, with the rise of AI, I believe it is very important to distinguish human text from machine-made; in my blog I will use AI only to fix grammatical and spelling mistakes. Thus I consider whatever is on here to be an extension and projection of my person, this is the start.
